## JTBD

### Core Job

When an experienced applicant is trying to turn a broad career history into a focused job-search system, they need a trustworthy way to choose the right roles, shape the right story, and reuse that work across applications without losing credibility.

### Functional Jobs

1. Decide whether a role is worth real effort.
2. Define which lane the role belongs to and what story that lane requires.
3. Gather company and market context that affects role fit, positioning, and timing.
4. Extract, organize, and approve the most relevant stories before drafting.
5. Produce assets and outreach from approved context.
6. Review what was used, missing, or excluded before sending.
7. Track application progress and learn what works by lane, source, company profile, and asset version.
8. Preserve supporting context such as reason-for-leaving, role transitions, and adjacent-lane framing for later reuse.

### Emotional and Social Jobs

- Feel less scattered and less reactive in a noisy market.
- Preserve professional credibility while using software assistance.
- Avoid the shame of sending generic, inflated, or weak-fit applications.
- Feel that setup work compounds instead of disappearing into one-off documents.

## Binding Requirements for Downstream Work

The following requirements are now binding unless a later governance reset overrides them:

1. `Free tier` and free-to-start clarity are trust requirements, not just pricing details.
2. No current-state promise may imply or suggest auto-apply.
3. Guided storytelling and guided story extraction are first-class product requirements, not polish.
4. Multiple role lanes must remain a first-class user need across brand, website, and app flows.
5. Company and market research should be treated as grounding inputs for later targeting and storytelling work.
6. Review-before-send and inspectable reasoning are trust architecture, not optional reassurance.
7. Application tracking and later A/B asset testing are real downstream product needs.
8. Reason-for-leaving capture is a downstream product and data need because it can affect positioning, transitions, and storytelling accuracy.
