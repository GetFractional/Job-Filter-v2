## Core Finding

The category usually captures demand by giving users immediate access to a working utility, not by asking for email first and promising vague future value.

That means the strongest lessons here are about product-led demand capture, not about generic newsletter growth.

## Best-in-Class Patterns

### Verified fact

- Careerflow makes free utility concrete before asking for upgrade intent.
- Jobright ties signup to a visible workflow, not just a list join.
- Teal and Simplify show how extension-led value can feel immediate.
- Rezi and Jobscan show how a strong free utility can earn trust before monetization.

### Inferred opportunity

- For Job Filter, the strongest demand-capture path is likely a clear job-search utility that immediately reduces noise or improves targeting quality.
- A lead magnet can work, but it should reinforce the main product story instead of replacing it.
- Subscriber capture should feel like an extension of product utility, not a detached content funnel.

### Not yet proven

- It is not yet proven whether a filtering-first entry point or a worksheet-first entry point converts better for Job Filter.
- It is not yet proven how much setup friction users will accept before seeing the first clear win.

## Weak or Generic Patterns to Avoid

| Pattern | Why it underperforms or drifts | Confidence |

| Static download with weak product handoff | Can grow the list without growing activation | Inferred opportunity |
| Auto-apply promise too early | Can create trust friction before the product earns the right to automate anything | Inferred opportunity |
| Credit-card-first trial | Adds risk before users understand product value | Verified fact |

## Normalized Implications for Job Filter

### Verified fact

- The category most often captures high-intent demand by letting users do something real after signup.
- Automation-heavy tools ask for resume plus job preferences before deeper activation.
- Content-led brands can grow lists, but they are solving a different core problem than product-led job-search tools.

### Inferred opportunity

- The `Job Profile Worksheet` should function as a secondary capture path, not the main front-door promise.
- Subscriber capture should be tied to a useful next step, such as saved filters, digests, or method previews, rather than a generic newsletter ask.
- A filtering or targeting utility can be a stronger first-touch wedge than generic application volume promises.

### Not yet proven

- The ideal placement for the worksheet in the funnel still belongs to later website work.
- The right balance between extension-led demand capture and worksheet-led demand capture is not yet proven.
- A content-led nurture path without product adjacency is unlikely to be strong, but the exact threshold for usefulness still needs validation.
