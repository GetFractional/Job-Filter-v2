# Customer JTBD And Persona Brief

Last updated: 2026-04-23

## Primary Market

People trying to build better careers by pursuing better opportunities with more clarity, proof, and control.

Job Filter should not brand itself as a tool only for one narrow identity group. Broad-history users remain an important wedge, but they are not the whole market.

## Best-Fit Active Buyer

People in or near an active search who need to:

- choose better-fit roles
- prove fit credibly
- keep applications moving without trusting black-box AI
- turn setup work into reusable leverage

## Market Layers

| Layer | Who it includes | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad market | Anyone trying to pursue the right next role with more clarity and control. | This is the brand frame. |
| Best-fit active buyer | Searchers who care more about fit quality than application volume. | This is the near-term growth center. |
| Wedge segments | Burnt-out applicants, skeptics, broad-history explorers, climbers, switchers, returners, signal-builders. | These are the first high-value stories and product fits. |
| Later expansion | Passive search, internal mobility, coach-assisted search. | These come after the proof-first loop is strong. |

## Primary JTBD

When I find or consider a role, help me quickly decide whether it is worth pursuing, prove my fit using real experience, and prepare reviewed materials I can trust.

## Five Uncertainties

| Uncertainty | User question | Product response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target | Which roles should I pursue? | Role Discovery and Role Lanes. |
| Fit | Can I credibly do this job? | Requirements matrix and job fit review. |
| Proof | What evidence supports my fit? | Verified Profile, claims, evidence, provenance. |
| Story | How do I explain the fit? | Role-specific story and reviewed assets. |
| Execution | What should I do next? | Applications, Workspace, next actions, follow-up. |

## Persona 1: Burnt-Out Applicant

### Situation

They have been applying for too many roles, rewriting materials repeatedly, and getting weak feedback from the market.

### Core anxiety

`I am working hard, but I cannot tell whether the work is improving my odds.`

### What they need

- a way to apply to fewer better roles
- evidence that setup work becomes reusable
- a clear next action
- fewer blank-page writing tasks
- less duplicate work across applications

### Success criteria

- verifies a reusable Profile
- rejects or holds weak-fit roles without guilt
- sends fewer but stronger applications
- can explain why each active application is worth the effort

### Copy that resonates

- `Stop rebuilding your story from scratch for every job.`
- `See which roles are worth your time before you write another version.`
- `Keep one reusable source of truth for applications you can defend.`

## Persona 2: Sophisticated Skeptic

### Situation

They have tried AI tools, resume scanners, and templates. They know some are useful, but they distrust unsupported claims and simplistic scores.

### Core anxiety

`If the tool makes me sound better than my evidence, I am the one who pays for it in the interview.`

### What they need

- visible source lineage
- confidence states
- review-before-send
- reversible suggestions
- clear separation between verified, inferred, missing, excluded, and unsupported

### Success criteria

- sees where every generated claim came from
- can exclude claims and proof safely
- trusts the app to show uncertainty instead of hiding it
- can export or delete their data

### Copy that resonates

- `No claim moves forward until you approve it.`
- `See what was used, what is missing, and what was left out.`
- `Use AI for structure, not fiction.`

## Persona 3: Broad-History Role Explorer

### Situation

They can credibly move in more than one direction. Their challenge is not having too little experience. It is deciding which story to lead with.

### Core anxiety

`I can do several things, but I do not know which version of me this market will believe.`

### What they need

- Role Lanes that group credible directions
- comparison between lanes
- supported and missing proof per lane
- opportunity-cost visibility
- help deciding what not to pursue

### Success criteria

- creates one or more user-owned Role Lanes
- sees which lanes have proof density
- understands gaps before applying
- can explain the lane choice in resumes, outreach, and interviews

### Copy that resonates

- `Find the roles your real experience can support.`
- `Compare paths before you spend another night rewriting.`
- `Turn a broad background into a focused application story.`

## Persona 4: Focused Climber

### Situation

They know the role, title, or level they want next. Their problem is not lack of direction. It is proving that their current body of work really supports the jump.

### Core anxiety

`I know where I want to go, but I am not sure my materials make the case clearly enough.`

### What they need

- a credible fit review against target roles
- help prioritizing the strongest opportunities
- proof-backed positioning for the next step up
- clear gaps to address without guesswork

### Success criteria

- sees which target roles have the strongest support
- can explain the promotion or transition case with evidence
- stops spending time on roles that only look good on paper

### Copy that resonates

- `See which next-step roles your work already supports.`
- `Find the strongest proof for the move you want to make.`
- `Know what strengthens your case before you apply.`

## Persona 5: Signal Builder

### Situation

They have thinner, newer, or uneven work history. They might be early-career, re-entering the market, or piecing together internships, projects, freelance work, or adjacent experience.

### Core anxiety

`I have signal, but I do not know how to package it without sounding inflated.`

### What they need

- help turning smaller experiences into credible proof
- explicit separation between strong evidence and thin evidence
- guidance on which opportunities are realistic now
- trust that the app will not overstate their story

### Success criteria

- understands which roles are realistic versus aspirational
- can reuse verified signal across multiple opportunities
- avoids unsupported claims while still telling a strong story

### Copy that resonates

- `Turn real signal into a story you can defend.`
- `See which opportunities fit the proof you already have.`
- `Build momentum without overstating your experience.`

## Buying Triggers

- job loss or expected transition
- stalled search after many applications
- promotion or title pivot
- first serious search after school, internship, or project work
- needing to explain broad, fractional, consulting, or cross-functional work
- return to market after a break
- distrust of resume keyword tools
- desire to apply to fewer roles without feeling passive

## Objections

| Objection | Product answer |
| --- | --- |
| I do not want another tracker. | The tracker is not the product center. The center is fit, proof, and reviewed action. |
| AI tools invent things. | Unsupported claims are labeled and blocked from downstream use until approved. |
| Setup sounds like work. | Setup becomes reusable leverage across roles, assets, outreach, and interviews. |
| I need to move fast. | The first win is verified Profile + first fit decision, not a perfect archive. |
| I already have a resume builder. | Job Filter decides what belongs in the resume and why, before formatting it. |
| I can use spreadsheets. | Spreadsheets track status, but they do not explain fit, proof, risk, or story. |

## Non-Goals

- applying to the most jobs
- hiding uncertainty behind a score
- replacing human judgment
- coaching users into exaggerated claims
- turning the app into a heavy CRM
- making resume templates the center of gravity

## Customer Language To Prefer

- apply with confidence
- roles worth your time
- prove your fit
- real experience
- reviewed application
- source evidence
- what to use, what is missing, what to leave out
- fewer better applications
- better opportunities for your career
- what this opportunity is really worth

## Customer Language To Avoid

- winning lane
- job-search operating system
- turbocharge
- 10x your job search
- dream job faster
- ATS hack
- recruiter-ready in seconds
- auto-apply
- done-for-you submission

## Sequencing Note

The brand should speak to the full career-building market now. The earliest product slices should still prioritize users who can provide enough real experience, project work, or adjacent proof to make the first review loop useful.
