# Customer Ascension Funnel

Last updated: 2026-04-23

## Purpose

Define how Job Filter should speak to the full market, convert the best-fit early users, and sequence the offer ladder without diluting the proof-first wedge.

## Strategy Frameworks Used

This funnel uses a narrow set of frameworks from the local `alen-sultanic` guidance:

- Simplification Hook
- Criteria of Markets
- 3 Levels of Customers
- Ascension Framework
- Belief Shifting
- Change Work

## Market Model

### Layer 1: Broad market

People trying to build a better career by pursuing better opportunities with more clarity, proof, and control.

### Layer 2: Best-fit active buyers

People in or near an active search who:

- care more about fit quality than application volume
- want reusable setup rather than repeated rewriting
- distrust black-box AI claims
- need a visible decision process, not just a resume tool

### Layer 3: Sharp wedge segments

- burnt-out applicants
- sophisticated skeptics
- broad-history role explorers
- focused climbers
- switchers and returners
- signal-builders with thinner or uneven work history

### Layer 4: Future expansion

- passive opportunity monitoring
- internal mobility and promotion planning
- team or coach-assisted search workflows
- career-market intelligence for repeat searches

## Message Hierarchy

### Brand frame

`Pursue the best opportunities for your career with clarity, proof, and control.`

### Product anchor

`Apply with confidence to roles you can prove you fit.`

### Mechanism

`Build a reusable profile from real experience, review what supports each opportunity, and move forward only when the story holds up.`

### Trust promise

`No auto-apply. Review before send. Source evidence stays visible.`

## What The Funnel Must Sell

The funnel is not selling a tracker, a resume template gallery, or faster form-filling.

It is selling a tighter chain of cause and effect:

1. organize real experience into reusable proof
2. evaluate opportunities before heavy application work
3. make fit and risk visible
4. approve only the claims that hold up
5. carry that proof into applications, outreach, and later career moves

## Simplification Hook

The market is overloaded with speed promises. Job Filter should simplify around one outcome:

`Know which opportunities are worth your time, and what proof actually supports them.`

That simplification should be visible on:

- Home hero
- How It Works step sequence
- Pricing free-first value
- worksheet and diagnostic offers
- onboarding activation milestones

## Funnel Stages

| Stage | User belief | What Job Filter must prove | Primary surfaces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | `I need a better way to manage my search.` | This is about better decisions, not more busywork. | Home, content, social proof, worksheet |
| Consideration | `Why not just use a tracker or resume tool?` | The product makes fit, proof, and risk visible before effort is wasted. | How It Works, teardown proof, FAQ, pricing |
| Activation | `Setup might be a lot of work.` | Setup becomes reusable leverage inside the first session. | Profile, first job review, onboarding |
| Conversion | `When should I pay?` | Pay only after visible value and repeat usage needs appear. | Pricing, upgrade moments, usage thresholds |
| Retention | `Why come back?` | The product improves every new opportunity review and application cycle. | Jobs, Review, Applications, Workspace |
| Expansion | `Can this support more of my career workflow?` | The same proof base can extend into assets, research, outreach, interviews, and later searches. | Workspace, future premium layers |

## Belief Shifts Required

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| `I should apply to more roles.` | `I should decide better and pursue stronger fits.` |
| `AI can write this for me.` | `AI should help structure only what I can defend.` |
| `My experience is messy.` | `My experience can be turned into reusable proof.` |
| `A tracker is enough.` | `I need fit, proof, and a clear next action.` |
| `This is only for broad-history senior people.` | `This helps anyone pursue the right opportunity from their real signal.` |

## Offer Ladder

### Free tier

Free must deliver a real first win:

- build Profile
- verify first usable history slice
- create at least one role direction or lane
- add or capture one job
- complete first apply, hold, or reject decision
- export basic data

### Core paid layer

Charge only after ongoing value is obvious. Candidate paid value:

- more active jobs, reviews, and applications
- richer grounded asset generation
- company and role research support
- extension capture and save convenience
- deeper proof coverage and history controls
- richer exports and outcome views

### Premium or later expansion

- advanced strategy and market intelligence
- interview and outreach systems from the same proof base
- opportunity monitoring and learning loops
- multi-search or coach-assisted workflows

## Page Jobs Inside The Funnel

| Surface | Funnel job |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Make the product category and trust model obvious. |
| How It Works | Show the mechanism from Profile to reviewed application. |
| Pricing | Make free-first value and upgrade logic credible. |
| Worksheet | Capture skeptical or not-ready visitors without dropping them into a dead-end lead magnet. |
| Profile onboarding | Turn setup into visible leverage fast. |
| First Review | Prove the product can save effort by deciding apply, hold, or reject. |

## Proof Assets Required

Before a public launch, the funnel should be able to show:

- one real product hero surface
- one fit review or requirements matrix view
- one proof coverage or source-lineage view
- one review-before-send state
- one workspace continuation state
- examples for at least four user types, not just broad-history operators

## Audience Sequencing Rule

The brand should speak to the full career-building market now. The earliest product slice should still prioritize people who can provide enough real work history to create a useful proof base quickly.

That means:

- broad public promise
- precise activation and beta targeting
- no pretending every persona is equally well served on day one

## Build Implications

- Homepage and launch copy must speak to opportunity choice, not only broad-history storytelling.
- Proof examples must include at least one focused climber and one thinner-history signal-builder case.
- Free-tier onboarding must create value without assuming seniority.
- Upgrade logic should trigger on repeated usage and deeper workflow needs, not on basic trust features.
