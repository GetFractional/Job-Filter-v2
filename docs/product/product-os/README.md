## Purpose

This package turns Job Filter's Foundation Series into a build, launch, measurement, and naming operating system.

Use it when deciding:

- what the product is
- who it helps
- why it is different
- what should be built first
- how screens should behave
- how proof and AI output stay safe
- how launch and QA gates work
- when brand naming can start

## Product Anchor

Brand frame:

`Pursue the best opportunities for your career with clarity, proof, and control.`

Application anchor:

`Apply with confidence to roles you can prove you fit.`

This replaces vague or markety internal phrasing like `winning lane` for customer-facing product work.

## Operating Rules

- ClickUp remains the scope gate.
- Foundation Series chapters remain upstream authority.
- Product OS docs can clarify, sequence, and package, but they cannot silently reopen locked Foundation decisions.
- Naming starts only after the PRD V3 thesis and category wedge are stable.
- Current-state product copy cannot imply auto-apply, hidden submission, or outcome certainty.
- Every generated or AI-assisted surface must expose source lineage and review-before-send.

## Required Review Lenses

- MECE product decomposition
- JTBD and customer anxieties
- first-principles uncertainty reduction
- design thinking from evidence to screen contracts
- RICE plus trust risk
- proof-grounding and review safety
- delivery governance and WIP discipline
