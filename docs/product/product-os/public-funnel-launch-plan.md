# Public Funnel Launch Plan

Last updated: 2026-04-23

## Purpose

Define the public funnel that supports the Product OS without reopening Chapter 04 authority.

## Funnel Thesis

The public site should make two things obvious:

`Job Filter helps you pursue the best opportunities for your career with clarity, proof, and control.`

`Job Filter helps you apply with confidence to roles you can prove you fit.`

The public funnel should not lead with a generic AI suite, resume template gallery, auto-apply promise, or dashboard tour.

The fuller audience and ascension logic lives in [`customer-ascension-funnel.md`](./customer-ascension-funnel.md).

## Page Set

| Page | Job | Primary CTA | Proof required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Establish relevance, differentiation, and first action. | `Build my job profile` | Product mechanism, trust boundaries, free start. |
| How It Works | Explain the workflow from Profile to reviewed application. | `Build my job profile` | Step-by-step mechanism and review safety. |
| Pricing | Explain Free tier and future paid value clearly. | `Start free` | No hidden export trap, upgrade after value. |
| Job Profile Worksheet | Capture secondary demand and help users preview the profile concept. | `Start my profile` | Worksheet maps to Profile, not a content download dead-end. |
| Auth | Create account or return to work. | `Continue setup` | Correct signed-in routing. |
| Privacy / Terms / 404 | Trust and recovery. | Contextual | Clear data and recovery path. |

## Home Page Content Model

1. Hero with product anchor and product UI proof.
2. Mechanism: Profile -> Role Discovery -> Review -> Workspace.
3. Trust boundaries: no auto-apply, review-before-send, source evidence.
4. Audience relevance: climbers, skeptical AI users, burnt-out applicants, broad-history explorers, and signal-builders.
5. Product preview: requirements matrix and proof coverage.
6. Free start and worksheet secondary path.
7. Footer.

## How It Works Content Model

1. Build a Profile from real experience.
2. Verify what can be reused.
3. Create Role Lanes.
4. Add or capture a job.
5. Review fit and proof.
6. Move qualified work to Applications.
7. Continue in Workspace.

## Pricing Content Model

### Free tier

Must include:

- build Profile
- verify history
- create first Role Lane
- add first job
- complete first fit decision
- export basic data

### Paid value candidates

Do not lock pricing yet. Candidate paid value:

- more active applications
- richer asset generation
- advanced company/research support
- extension capture
- advanced exports
- richer telemetry/outcome learning

### Pricing rules

- avoid fake urgency
- avoid paywalling trust-critical review
- avoid last-mile export traps
- make cancellation and data portability clear

## Worksheet Page

The worksheet is secondary capture, not the main product story.

Fields:

- roles I am considering
- roles I do not want anymore
- work history that is hard to explain
- strongest proof points
- recent applications that stalled
- what I need help deciding
- email

Follow-up:

- send worksheet summary
- invite `Build my job profile`
- optionally route to beta waitlist

## Beta Launch Plan

### Segment

Start with users who can reach value quickly in the current product slice:

- operators
- individual contributors pushing for a clearer next role
- consultants
- fractional leaders
- career switchers with adjacent proof
- cross-functional managers
- people returning after layoff
- early-career professionals with internships, projects, or freelance signal strong enough to review credibly

### Promise

`Bring one real role. Leave knowing whether it is worth applying to and what proof supports your story.`

### Beta success criteria

- 70% complete source/profile start
- 50% reach first fit decision
- 30% create a reviewed application workspace
- unsupported-claim export rate = 0
- qualitative confidence lift from baseline to after Review

## Launch Experiments

| Experiment | Hypothesis | Segment | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home hero promise | Career-opportunity framing plus `Apply with confidence` beats speed/resume-led copy. | All visitors | CTA click rate and auth start. |
| Worksheet secondary CTA | Secondary worksheet captures skeptical visitors. | Non-signup visitors | Worksheet completion. |
| Free first win | Showing free first fit decision improves signup trust. | Pricing visitors | Pricing -> auth start. |
| Proof-first demo | Requirements matrix demo improves activation intent. | Home/How visitors | Demo engagement and signup. |
| Segment proof blocks | Showing examples for climbers, explorers, and signal-builders improves relevance. | Qualified visitors | Signup rate by segment. |

## Launch Assets

- Home page
- How It Works page
- Pricing page
- Worksheet page
- beta invite copy
- onboarding emails after signup
- support article for proof and review states
- FAQ content inside core pages

## Launch Guardrails

- no outcome guarantees
- no auto-apply claims
- no hidden AI action claims
- no unsupported user-count or success-rate claims
- no screenshots with fake private user data
- all example claims labeled as examples
