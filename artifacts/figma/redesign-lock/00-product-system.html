Lane system

Use multiple lanes when one profile can credibly pursue adjacent role categories without flattening everything into one generic asset set.

Each lane should keep its own role-title focus, fit evidence, active jobs, selected assets, and experiment history. Fit signals should never appear without the proof behind them.

Why multiple lanes
Lane purpose

Separate credible role categories so the user can sort jobs, keep different title families visible, and avoid collapsing everything into one generic resume path.

Proof behind fit

Whenever a fit signal appears, show the proof that created it, such as approved stories, matching titles, grounded company context, and current missing-proof count.

Kanban view treats each lane like a swimlane. Jobs sit inside the lane they belong to, and each lane keeps its own asset and experiment context.
