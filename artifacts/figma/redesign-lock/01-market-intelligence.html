Locked Persona Stack

Burnt-Out Applicant

Experienced, capable, and tired of wasting effort on low-signal roles and low-trust tools.

Primary fear

More work with no better outcomes.

What Job Filter must prove

The product reduces waste, improves role targeting, and compounds prior work into a usable search system instead of creating more admin.

Implication for downstream work

Effort-to-value ratio, reusable leverage, and free-to-start clarity need to stay visible from entry through workflow use.

Verified fact
Sophisticated Skeptic

High-agency operator with some manual system already in place and a low tolerance for black-box AI.

Define the functional, emotional, and social work the product is really being hired to do.

This market-intelligence layer keeps the job definition broader than drafting. The real job is choosing better roles, shaping a credible story, and sustaining a search without losing trust or coherence.

Core job

When an experienced applicant is trying to turn a broad career history into a focused job-search system, they need a trustworthy way to choose the right roles, build the right lanes, search the right jobs, shape the right story, and reuse that work across applications without losing credibility.

Functional jobs
  1. 01Decide whether a role is worth real effort.
  2. 02Define the right lanes, role titles, and story expectations for each search path.
  3. 03Search for jobs associated with those lanes, then gather company and market context that affects fit, positioning, and timing.
  4. 04Extract, organize, and approve the most relevant stories before drafting.
  5. 05Produce assets and outreach from approved context.
  6. 06Review what was used, missing, or excluded before sending.
  7. 07Track application progress and learn what works by lane, source, company profile, and asset version.
  8. 08Preserve supporting context such as reason-for-leaving, role transitions, and adjacent-lane framing for later reuse.
Emotional and social jobs
  1. 01Feel less scattered and less reactive in a noisy market.
  2. 02Preserve professional credibility while using software assistance.
  3. 03Avoid the shame of sending generic, inflated, or weak-fit applications.
  4. 04Feel that setup work compounds instead of disappearing into one-off documents.

Funnel Benchmark Logic and Lane Implications

This market-intelligence layer does not turn benchmark bands into promises. It records what can safely be used later for user education, tracking logic, and lane-aware analytics design.

Benchmark signal Why it matters for Job Filter Evidence quality
Application-to-interview conversion should be read as a lane-level range, not one universal number. Different role families, channels, and markets behave differently. Verified fact

Suggested lane-level benchmark schema
  • Job-seeker lane: role function, seniority, geography, industry target, work model.
  • Application lane: channel, company size, job type, remote or hybrid status.
  • Funnel stages: submitted, responded, screened, interviewed, final, offer, accepted, rejected, ghosted.
  • Learning overlays: asset version, lane, company profile, reason-for-leaving context.
