Activation System, Mobile

Mobile reference for activation and core app surfaces. This document carries the same step grammar, trust rules, and CTA system as the desktop workspace while adapting them to touch-sized, contained mobile flows.

Purpose

Show the mobile expression of route gate, setup, feeds, assets, and trust-review surfaces.

Scope In

  • Touch layout and hierarchy
  • State continuity
  • Signed-in progression language

Scope Out

  • New app scope
  • Production implementation
  • Alternative CTA systems

Locked Decisions

Signed-in continuation remains `Continue setup`. Feed-first and Why this draft remain intact.

Pending Proof / Open Items

Figma child-node verification remains pending and premium handoff naming remains intentionally open.

Review Checklist

No legacy file-letter naming, no chip-led summary header, and no loss of the calm premium workspace feel.
