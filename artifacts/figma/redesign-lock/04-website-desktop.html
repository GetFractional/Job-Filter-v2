Mechanism

One operating loop from role choice to final review.

Job Filter is not another pile of prompts, docs, and guesswork. The workflow stays in one place: choose the lane, build the Profile, inspect the reasoning, then move only when the story feels defensible. That is the simplification hook. It removes needless decisions without hiding the work.

What changes

The cost of disconnected tools is hidden until the story breaks.

Fragmented job-search stacks make it easy to lose the role logic, overstate experience, or send a draft that sounds generic. Job Filter keeps the inputs, lane decisions, and review path connected so the next application starts from clearer truth instead of fresh chaos.

  • Role lanes stay visible instead of disappearing into one generic resume.
  • Guided storytelling happens before asset formatting, not after.
  • Review-before-send is part of the workflow, not a reassuring footnote.
